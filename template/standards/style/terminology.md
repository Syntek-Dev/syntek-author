> **This file is a seeded stub, and it is deliberately unfinished.** It ships with the project so
> that the skills which route here point at something real from day one. Until the author
> confirms a term (usually at the end of a grilling session), the table below has no rows.

The single home for words the work uses in one fixed sense. `grill-with-docs` records a term
here once the author has settled it, and `learn-voice` once the author approves a term it found
in the ledger; `spelling`, `comprehension` and `flow` read it; a term used in two senses across
units is the defect this file exists to prevent.

Each row has four parts: **Term** (exactly as written in the work), **Meaning** (one sentence,
in this work's sense), **Use** (where and how it appears) and **Avoid** (the near-synonyms that
must not stand in for it, and why).

A contested term records the reading this work adopts in **Meaning** and names the other
readings in **Use**; the full map lives in `research/src/contested-readings/`.
