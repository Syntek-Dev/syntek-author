# BUSINESS.md — teach, business mode

The families a business writer learns in, and the house sources each practises against.

## Paths and unit

- **Workspace:** `learning/<topic>/`. Practice documents imitate a document in `library/src/`,
  but never sit there, are never registered and are never given a `DOC-NNN`.
- **House sources:** `standards/method/BUSINESS.md`, `standards/brand/`, the guides in
  `library/docs/reference/` and `planning/docs/reference/`, and the template instruments in each
  family's `templates/` folder under `library/src/`.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 1 — also record the family:** document craft, process, or domain.
- **Step 3 — also,** a domain lesson's primary source is the law, the regulator's guidance or the
  standard itself, through `research`, never a summary of it; note the jurisdiction and the date
  checked.
- **Step 4 — also,** every practice piece uses invented parties (for example 'Example Client Ltd'
  and 'Harbour Bakery') and invented figures. **Never a real client, and never a real client's
  numbers.**

## Domain rules

| Family | What is practised | House source |
|---|---|---|
| Document craft | stating a rule and bounding its exception; a scope boundary; a one-page summary that carries the decision; a fee table that sums; defined terms defined once; the house voice; LaTeX deliverables; correspondence anatomy | `standards/method/BUSINESS.md`; `standards/brand/brand-voice.md`; `library/docs/reference/document-anatomy.md`; `library/docs/reference/latex-deliverables.md` |
| Process | adding, reversioning and superseding a register row; review cycles and approvals; versioning | `planning/docs/reference/the-document-register.md`; `library/docs/reference/versioning-and-the-register.md` |
| Domain | data protection, sector conventions, tax basics, information-security standards: each from its primary source | the note `research` writes, cited and dated |

- **A practice piece carries no version number** and sits only under `LESSONS/`, so it is never
  mistaken for a real draft or a template.
- **Never practise on a live client matter.** Invent the party; a practice document naming a real
  client will eventually be read as real.

## Examples

- **Mission (document craft):** 'Write a liability clause I could defend line by line.' First
  lesson: a rule and its bounded exception, for an invented cleaning contract.
- **Mission (process):** 'Supersede a document without losing its history.' First lesson: a
  practice register table in `LESSONS/`, taking an invented policy from v1.0 to v1.1.
