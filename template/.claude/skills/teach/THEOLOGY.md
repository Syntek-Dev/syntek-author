# THEOLOGY.md — teach, theology mode

The families a theology author learns in, and the house sources each practises against.

## Paths and unit

- **Workspace:** `learning/<topic>/`. Practice passages imitate a section of a chapter in
  `manuscript/src/`, but never sit there.
- **House sources:** `standards/method/THEOLOGY.md` and `standards/method/method.md`; the guides
  in `manuscript/docs/reference/`, `planning/docs/reference/` and `research/docs/reference/`;
  `standards/style/voice-notes.md`.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 1 — also record the family:** theology and method, craft, evidence, or publishing.
- **Step 3 — also,** a lesson on a passage of scripture cites the text in the project's default
  translation (the style sheet names it) and, for an original-language point, a standard lexicon
  or grammar through `research`. Never teach a word's meaning from memory.
- **Step 4 — also,** a practice argument uses a question nobody in the work is answering, so the
  practice cannot leak into a chapter's position.

## Domain rules

| Family | What is practised | House source |
|---|---|---|
| Theology and method | the six claim categories, labelling sentences and catching silent collapses; stating a contested reading so its holders would recognise it; conceding before rebutting; steelmanning | `standards/method/THEOLOGY.md` Sections 1 to 6; `research/docs/reference/contested-readings.md`; `planning/docs/reference/argument-maps.md` |
| Craft | the anatomy of a section; what belongs in the main text and what in a footnote; holding one voice across sections drafted out of order | `manuscript/docs/reference/section-anatomy.md`; `manuscript/docs/reference/main-text-and-footnotes.md`; `standards/style/voice-notes.md` |
| Evidence | the basis a claim needs; reading a study for what it was not about; the six verdicts | `standards/method/method.md` Sections 2 to 7; `research/docs/reference/vetting-evidence.md` |
| Publishing | where a proposal is in hand: its anatomy, comparable titles, approaching an endorser | the proposal layer's guides, where the project has one |

- **Drills use invented questions and invented churches.** A practice map of a contested reading
  may use a real passage, because the passage is public; it may not adopt a position for the
  book.
- **Never practise on testimony or first-person material** entrusted to the author.

## Examples

- **Mission (theology and method):** 'Label my own paragraphs by claim category without help,
  so category-check finds nothing I could have caught.' First lesson: telling interpretive
  inference from the author's theological conclusion, on three invented sentences.
- **Mission (craft):** 'Write a concession a holder of the other view would sign.' First lesson:
  the recognition test, practised on a view about church music the book never discusses.
