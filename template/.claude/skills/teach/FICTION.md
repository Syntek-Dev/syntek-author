# FICTION.md — teach, fiction mode

The families a novelist learns in, and the house sources each practises against.

## Paths and unit

- **Workspace:** `learning/<topic>/`. Practice scenes imitate a section of a chapter in
  `manuscript/src/`, but never sit there, and never use the book's characters.
- **House sources:** `standards/method/FICTION.md`; the guides in `manuscript/docs/reference/`,
  `planning/docs/reference/`, `world/docs/reference/` and `research/docs/reference/`;
  `standards/style/voice-notes.md` and `standards/style/style-sheet.md`.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 1 — also record the family:** craft, structure, research, or publishing.
- **Step 4 — also,** a practice scene uses invented people in an invented place: never the book's
  cast, so nothing practised is mistaken for canon, and never a name the register already holds.
- **Step 4 — also,** where the lesson is about an invented language, practise in a throwaway
  language made for the lesson inside `LESSONS/`, never in one of the book's languages; the
  lesson's facts about real languages come from `research`, cited.

## Domain rules

| Family | What is practised | House source |
|---|---|---|
| Craft | scene goal, conflict and outcome; point-of-view discipline; dialogue and its punctuation; restraint with invented words on the page | `manuscript/docs/reference/scene-craft.md`; `standards/method/FICTION.md` Sections 3 and 5; `standards/style/style-sheet.md` |
| Structure | 'because' and 'therefore' chains; want against need and the arc; setup and payoff; pacing | `planning/docs/reference/causality-chains.md`; `planning/docs/reference/character-arcs.md`; `standards/method/FICTION.md` Sections 1, 2 and 4 |
| Research | real-world detail and deliberate departures; representation researched from a community's own voices; permissions for quoted material; with the language kit, sound, IPA and sound change | `research/docs/reference/real-world-detail.md`; `standards/risk/FICTION.md`; `world/docs/reference/naming.md`; the language guides in `world/docs/reference/`, where installed |
| Publishing | where a submission package is in hand: the query letter, the synopsis, comparable titles | the proposal layer's guides, where the project has one |

- **Invented everything.** A lesson never reuses the book's people, places, languages or plot,
  because a practice version that turns out well tempts a shortcut into the real book.

## Examples

- **Mission (structure):** 'Plot a chapter where every beat has a cause, before I draft it.'
  First lesson: rewriting three 'and then' beats about an invented lighthouse keeper as
  'because' and 'therefore'.
- **Mission (research, language kit):** 'Hear the difference between two sound models before I
  choose one for my desert people.' First lesson: transcribing six invented words in IPA, with
  the symbols' values taken from a cited reference, not from memory.
