# THEOLOGY.md — approach-a-reader: prospective endorsers

The theology mode of `approach-a-reader`. The reader is a prospective **endorser**: someone whose
name on the cover or in the proposal tells a particular audience the book is worth their time.
`SKILL.md` owns the procedure; this file owns the paths, the tiers, the length and the chase rule.

## Paths and unit

- **The unit** is one approach email to one named person.
- **The tracker:** `proposal/src/endorsements/tracker.md`, with the columns Name · Affiliation ·
  Tier · Why them · The ask · Sent · Approached · Status · Reply · Next action · Due, and the
  statuses `to approach` · `approached` · `chasing` · `yes` · `no` · `lapsed`.
- **Drafts:** `proposal/src/endorsements/drafts/<reader-slug>.md`, one per person.
- **Received endorsements:** `proposal/src/endorsements/received-<reader-slug>.md`, verbatim.
- **The master text:** the book in a paragraph and the hook in a sentence, from
  `proposal/src/book-proposal/01-overview-and-hook.md`; the author's standing from
  `proposal/src/book-proposal/06-about-the-author.md`.
- **Extra reads:** `proposal/docs/reference/book-proposal-anatomy.md` (endorsers, by reach) and
  `standards/method/THEOLOGY.md` rule 8 (bias declared, not neutralised).

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 1 — also** check the row's tier and status; a drafted email leaves the row at
  `to approach`.
- **Step 2 — also** place the person in a tier, in approach order: **scholars in the book's field**
  (their yes says the book is serious; approach first); **practitioners** who can vouch for its
  subject-matter claims; **church-institutional voices**, always through a named person, never a
  department; **voices beyond the church**, only if crossover is pursued, and told early that the
  book is Christian. Name which objection their yes answers: where `.claude/MEMORY.md` records the
  author's stake, a practitioner who can judge the field answers 'you would say that' better than
  a bigger name who cannot.
- **Step 3 — also** the ask is one the author can honour: a short endorsement, or a read of named
  chapters, each already promoted or with an agreed date.
- **Step 5 — also** keep it **under 250 words**: the book in two sentences, leading with the hook;
  the author's stake in **one clause**, declared plainly rather than hedged or performed; the ask
  with the reading involved, roughly how long it takes and the date it is needed by; a sentence
  that makes declining easy and costless.
- **Step 6 — also** never name another endorser until their endorsement is received and they have
  agreed to be named, and never imply a publisher is attached when none is.
- **Step 10 — also** *Approached* is the date the author sent it. A reply is recorded as it came:
  'interested, will read in the spring' stays `approached` with a dated next action, not `yes`.
- **Step 11 — also** one chase, a fortnight after the approach, shorter than the original and with
  an easier exit; the row moves to `chasing`, and if nothing comes, to `lapsed`. After a yes, the
  author sends what was promised by the date promised, and the endorsement is kept verbatim in its
  received file.

## Domain rules

- **Reach, not prestige.** Ask whose yes unlocks which audience; a reader who can judge the book's
  claims is worth more than a famous name who cannot.
- **Candour about the author's position.** A theologian will see a conflict of interest at once;
  being told plainly, in one clause, is what makes the approach credible rather than awkward.
- **What an endorser vouches for is what the book claims about itself.** The approach never
  overstates the argument: it describes the book the method holds it to be
  (`standards/method/THEOLOGY.md`), not a stronger one.
- **The church's institutions are approached through people.** A cold message to a department or
  a general inbox wastes the chance.
- **Endorsements are kept verbatim.** An endorsement is never edited, shortened or improved
  without the endorser's agreement to the new wording.

## Examples

Invented people and works, for shape only.

> **Opening, scholar tier:** 'Dear Dr Hale, your chapter on welcome in *A Theology of the
> Returning Guest* is the reason I am writing: my book picks up where it stops, with what a church
> owes the visitor who comes back.'

> **The stake, in one clause:** '…written by someone who has kept a church's welcome rota for ten
> years, and says so on the first page.'

> **Tracker row, after sending:** `| Ruth Hale | [college] | Scholars in the field | Her chapter on
> welcome frames the book's argument | 80-word endorsement | Chapters 1 and 3 | 14/11/2026 |
> approached | — | Chase if no reply | 28/11/2026 |`
