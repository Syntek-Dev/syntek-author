# THEOLOGY.md — handoff, theology mode

What a theology book's handoff must never drop: the standing commitments that cut across every
chapter, and are the easiest thing to lose at a session boundary.

## Paths and unit

- **Unit:** a chapter, `manuscript/src/NN-kebab-title/`, with its sections in `drafts/`; its brief
  is `planning/src/units/NN-kebab-title.md` and its argument map
  `planning/src/arguments/NN-kebab-title.md`.
- **Anchors** for in-flight work point into the section draft, the brief's
  `## Claims and categories`, the argument map, or a contested-reading map in
  `research/src/contested-readings/`.

## Additions to the steps

- **Step 1 — also** record any reading settled this session in its contested-reading map, and any
  claim's category in the brief, before writing the handoff.
- **Step 5 — also carry the standing commitments.** The part is headed `## Standing commitments`
  and names, whenever the session touched a chapter:
  - **The objection the book leaves standing** (`standards/method/THEOLOGY.md` Section 7): which
    it is, or that it is still undesignated, and whether this session's work came near answering
    it.
  - **The bias disclosure** (Section 8 of the same standard): whether the chapter in flight needs
    it restated, and where.
  - **Open claims:** sections drafted this session whose claim categories are not yet labelled,
    and every `VERIFY` flag opened on a scripture reference, a translation or an original-language
    point.
- **Step 7 — also** name `category-check` or `steelman` next when a chapter is close to structural
  review.

## Domain rules

- **Chapter drafting is costly to lose to compaction.** A half-drafted chapter whose contested
  reading was settled, whose concession was positioned and whose figures were verified holds many
  resolved decisions a summary will flatten. Hand off rather than compact.
- **Confidential first-person material** (a pastoral conversation, a church's internal matter) is
  named and located, never described, even in outline.
- **Never carry a reading in the handoff alone.** A reading the session adopted lives in its map
  and the brief; the handoff points to them.

## Examples

```markdown
## Standing commitments

- Objection left standing: 'rest as privilege' (the Decisions heading of .claude/MEMORY.md,
  03/10/2026). This session's section 03-sabbath-rest/drafts/04-the-gift.md:31 comes close to
  answering it; flagged for the author, not changed.
- Bias disclosure: restated in chapter 1 only; chapter 3 does not need it (brief, line 22).
- Open claims: drafts/04-the-gift.md has two claims without categories (lines 12, 40) and one
  VERIFY on the Hebrew of the commandment (line 18).
```
