`planning/docs/reference/causality-chains.md` explains the columns; `standards/method/FICTION.md` owns the rules.

## Writing rules

1. **IDs run `B001`, `B002` … in order of entry.**
   They are never renumbered or reused; a cut beat is marked `(cut)` in its row, never deleted.
2. **`Because` cites a cause:** a beat ID, a character's decision, or a rule of the world.
   'Then' is not a cause.
3. **Coincidence may cause trouble, never resolve it.**
   A coincidence the author keeps is recorded as a decision in `.claude/MEMORY.md`, so a later pass does not 'fix' it.
4. **`Where` is `<unit> · <section-slug>`**, or `planned` for a beat not yet drafted.
5. **Every setup appears under Open setups** until its payoff is charted.
6. **One sentence per cell.**
