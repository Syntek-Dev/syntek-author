Every fact the story has established, with the section that established it.
Prose that contradicts a row here is reported to the author, never silently repaired.
The `continuity` skill reads this ledger with the timeline, the world entries and the names register.

## Writing rules

1. **IDs run `F001`, `F002` …** and are never renumbered or reused.
2. **A fact enters when promoted prose establishes it, or when the author decides it.**
   A fact a skill proposes waits under Proposed until the author confirms it.
3. **`Established in` is `<unit> · <section-slug>`**, or `author` for a decided fact not yet on the page.
4. **`Kind` is one of** `character` · `place` · `object` · `rule` · `relationship` · `time`.
5. **A changed fact is superseded, never edited.**
   Mark the old row `(superseded by F0NN)` and add the new one, so the history of the change survives.
6. **One sentence per cell.**
