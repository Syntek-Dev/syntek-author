Every event of the story in the order it happens, which is not always the order it is told.
The causal order lives in `planning/src/causality.md`; established facts live in `planning/src/continuity.md`.

## Writing rules

1. **One row per event, in story-time order.**
   Flashbacks and reordered telling keep their place by when they happen, not by where they are told.
2. **`When` uses the story's own reckoning**, stated once under Calendar.
   Relative times ('day 3, dusk') are allowed when nothing fixes an absolute one.
3. **`Told in` is `<unit> · <section-slug>`**, or `planned`, or `untold` for an event that shapes the story off the page.
4. **`Beat` links the causality beat** where the event is one.
5. **Ages, distances, travel times and seasons are facts.**
   Once the prose establishes one, it also goes into `planning/src/continuity.md`.
6. **One sentence per cell.**

## Calendar

How time is reckoned in this story: the calendar, its months or seasons, and the date the story opens.
