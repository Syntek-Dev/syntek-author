> (V1, V2 …) and never restated here.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `standards/method/FICTION.md`. · _opus_
- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Read the outline, the causality chain, the arcs involved and the world entries the quest touches. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Checked for an existing quest file; if one exists, the author confirmed a redesign. · _sonnet_

## Execution Checklist

**The quest**

- [ ] Goal and stakes agreed with the author. · _opus_
- [ ] Trigger named, with the beat that causes it. · _opus_
- [ ] Obstacles and reversals listed in order, each against its chapter and causing beat. · _opus_

**Ties and rules**

- [ ] **Every creature, culture, place and rule the quest relies on checked against its entry in `world/src/`; conflicts reported.** · _opus_
- [ ] Cost and reward stated; an empty cost flagged. · _opus_
- [ ] Every arc the quest moves named, with its beat. · _opus_

**Writing**

- [ ] Ending chosen by the author, or the reason for leaving it open recorded. · _opus_
- [ ] Quest written at `planning/src/quests/<slug>.md`; undecided points flagged. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] **The author has agreed the quest, and it cannot be left dangling: it resolves or says why it stays open.** · _opus_
- [ ] Every tie points at a real arc, beat or world entry. · _sonnet_
