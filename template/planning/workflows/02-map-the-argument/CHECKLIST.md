# CHECKLIST.md — map a chapter's argument before drafting

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** `planning/docs/reference/argument-maps.md`, `standards/method/THEOLOGY.md` and the
> `argument-audit` and `category-check` skills. The map is what gates V4.1 and V4.2 later check
> the prose against. Gates are cited from `standards/verification/verification.md` by number (V1,
> V2 …) and never restated here.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `standards/method/THEOLOGY.md` in full. · _opus_
- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The chapter's brief exists at `outlined` or later and has been read. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Checked for an existing map; if one exists, the author confirmed a re-audit. · _sonnet_

## Execution Checklist

**Claims**

- [ ] Thesis agreed with the author in one sentence. · _opus_
- [ ] Every claim written as one sentence with an ID; compound claims split. · _opus_
- [ ] Every claim carries exactly one of the six categories; silent collapses flagged. · _opus_

**Support**

- [ ] Every claim names its support, or is listed under open moves. · _opus_
- [ ] **No reference, quotation or attribution supplied from memory; every unchecked one carries `VERIFY`.** · _opus_
- [ ] Every contested reading has an ID and a linked research entry, or the dependent claims are marked unsupported. · _opus_

**Objections and concessions**

- [ ] Every objection stated so its holders would recognise it, with who holds it and where it is answered or left standing. · _opus_
- [ ] Every concession placed in a section ahead of the section that answers it. · _opus_
- [ ] No objection designated as left standing without the author's word. · _opus_

**Writing**

- [ ] Map written at `planning/src/arguments/<unit>.md`, one sentence per line. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Agreed claims, IDs and categories carried into the brief's `## Claims and categories`. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] **Every claim has a category and named support, or is reported as an open move.** · _opus_
- [ ] Open moves reported to the author, blocking first; nothing in the argument rewritten. · _opus_
- [ ] The map and the brief agree on every claim ID. · _sonnet_
