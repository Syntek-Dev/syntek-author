## Purpose (one line)

Chart a unit's beats so each one has a cause, a consequence and a place in story time before it
is drafted.

## How to work here

- **Routing:** skill `causality`; guide `planning/docs/reference/causality-chains.md`; standard
  `standards/method/FICTION.md`. New facts are proposed for the `continuity` ledger, never
  entered as established.
- **Model:** **Opus** for every judgement about cause and consequence; the mechanical tier for
  adding agreed rows (`.claude/rules/syntek-author/05-model-allocation.md`).
- **Concrete steps:** follow `STEPS.md` in order and tick `CHECKLIST.md` as you go.
- **Definition of done:** every beat of the unit has a `Because` that is a real cause, every
  setup is paired or listed as open, and the author has agreed the chain.

## Guardrails

- **'Then' is not a cause.** A beat whose only cause is the previous beat in time is flagged
  until the author supplies a real one.
- **Coincidence may cause trouble, never resolve it.** Flag any beat where chance gets a
  character out; a kept coincidence is the author's recorded decision.
- **Report contradictions; never silently repair them.** If the chain disagrees with the
  timeline, the world or the prose, tell the author which disagrees with which.
- **IDs are permanent.** A cut beat is marked `(cut)`, never deleted or renumbered.
- **Never overwrite** a row without confirming with the author.

## Output & naming

- **Writes:** rows in `planning/src/causality.md`, `planning/src/timeline.md`, and proposed rows
  in `planning/src/continuity.md`; the brief's `## Continuity facts`.
- **Does not touch:** the prose in `manuscript/src/`, the world entries in `world/src/`, or any
  established continuity row.
