# CHECKLIST.md — chart a character's arc

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** `planning/docs/reference/character-arcs.md`, `standards/method/FICTION.md` and the
> `chart-character-arc` skill. Gates are cited from `standards/verification/verification.md` by
> number (V1, V2 …) and never restated here.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `standards/method/FICTION.md`. · _opus_
- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The character's entry exists in `world/src/characters/` and has been read. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Checked for an existing arc; if one exists, the author confirmed a re-chart. · _sonnet_

## Execution Checklist

**Shape**

- [ ] Arc type chosen by the author from options offered. · _opus_
- [ ] The truth, and the gap between want and need, stated by citing the entry, not copying it. · _opus_
- [ ] The turn identified, with why it breaks there. · _opus_

**Beats**

- [ ] Every shift listed in order against its chapter and section. · _opus_
- [ ] **Every shift tied to a causality beat, or sent to `03-chart-the-causality` first.** · _opus_

**Writing**

- [ ] Arc written at `planning/src/arcs/<slug>.md`; undecided items under `## Open`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The entry's `arc:` points at the arc. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] **The author has agreed the arc, and no shift lacks a section or a cause.** · _opus_
- [ ] The hand-back names the briefs that carry the arc's shifts. · _opus_
