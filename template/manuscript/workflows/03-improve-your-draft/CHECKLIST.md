# CHECKLIST.md — improve the author's draft

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>

> **See** `manuscript/docs/reference/drafting-with-ai.md` and the `improve-section` skill with its

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Draft located in the chapter's drafts folder, with frontmatter and a ledger entry (created if missing, `origin: author`). · _sonnet_
- [ ] Strength agreed with the author, or `light` used and said so. · _opus_
- [ ] `standards/style/voice-notes.md`, the style sheet and the `improve-section` mode file read. · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

**Reading**

- [ ] Section read against its purpose in the brief; gaps and extras noted. · _opus_

**Passes, in order**

- [ ] Structural proposals made at `rework` only, with the argument or events and the one job intact. · _opus_
- [ ] Line pass at `edit` and `rework`: voice markers, deliberate oddities and honest hedges kept. · _opus_
- [ ] Mechanics pass: `grammar` then `spelling`, recurring items grouped, no comment on the author. · _opus_

**The diff**

- [ ] Every proposal numbered, located, shown before and after, with a one-line reason. · _opus_
- [ ] **Nothing outside the lane proposed: figures, dates, citation keys, quotations, commitments and the argument raised only as questions.** · _opus_
- [ ] The author answered: all, none, or by number. · _opus_

**Applying and recording**

- [ ] Only accepted proposals applied; no meaning changed. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Every proposal logged in the ledger, accepted or rejected, with the author's words where given. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Draft at `status: improved`, `last_updated` set, status mirrored in the brief. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] **The author saw every proposal with its reason before anything changed, and only accepted changes were applied.** · _opus_
- [ ] Every proposal is in the ledger. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The draft is still in drafts and nothing was promoted. · _opus_
