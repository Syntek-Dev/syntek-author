## Execution Checklist

**Objections**

- [ ] Every objection listed with its location, implicit ones included; any in the map but missing from the prose noted. · _opus_
- [ ] **Recognition test** applied to each, with `tradition-check` for how its holders would put it. · _opus_
- [ ] **Ease test** applied: anything dispatched in a sentence flagged. · _opus_
- [ ] **Omission test** applied: the strongest unmentioned objection named. · _opus_
- [ ] **The objection aimed at the author confirmed at full strength, and the author's stake declared in the body.** Blocking if missing. · _opus_

**Concessions and readings**

- [ ] Every concession checked by position: in the body, in its own passage, before the response, in its holders' terms. · _opus_
- [ ] Any same-sentence rebuttal or footnoted cost flagged as not conceded. · _opus_
- [ ] Every contested passage mapped, and named in the body with the adopted reading and what would change. · _opus_

**Categories, standing objections, posture**

- [ ] Answers to objections checked for category slips with `category-check`. · _opus_
- [ ] Chapter swept for any answer to an objection left standing; if answered, reported and stopped. · _opus_
- [ ] Posture check: nothing that would leave a reader who decides differently defensive. · _opus_

**Report**

- [ ] Prioritised report delivered, blocking first, each item with location, failed check, reason, suggested fix and owner. · _opus_
- [ ] **Three verdicts stated explicitly, even where they pass.** · _opus_
- [ ] Nothing rewritten: no argument, no conclusion, no response softened. · _opus_

## Done When

- [ ] Every objection passes the recognition test or is flagged with a stronger version suggested. · _opus_
- [ ] Every concession sits ahead of its rebuttal in the body, or is flagged as blocking. · _opus_
- [ ] Contested readings are in the body. · _opus_
- [ ] Anything left standing is confirmed intact, or is with the author. · _opus_
- [ ] The three verdicts are on the record, and every fix has an owner. · _opus_
