The single source of truth for who has been asked to endorse the book, what they were asked and sent, and what came back.
Kept by `proposal/workflows/03-update-the-tracker/`; read before every approach.

## Rules

- **Status:** `to approach` · `approached` · `chasing` · `yes` · `no` · `lapsed`.
- A drafted email is not an approach.
  The status stays `to approach` until the author sends it, and *Approached* is the date the author sent it.
- A reply is recorded as it came and never upgraded: 'interested, will read in the spring' is `approached` with a dated next action, not `yes`.
- Every open row has a next action and its date; closed rows are `yes`, `no` or `lapsed`.
- One chase, a fortnight after the approach; if nothing comes, `lapsed`.
- An endorsement received is stored verbatim in `received-<reader-slug>.md` beside this file.
- Dates are DD/MM/YYYY.
- Corrections are appended below with a date; history is never overwritten.
