# CHECKLIST.md — update the tracker

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** the tracker's own `## Rules` and the package anatomy guide in `proposal/docs/reference/`.
> No gate in `standards/verification/verification.md` applies; the tracker's truth is the gate.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] **Read the tracker:** found the row, or confirmed there is none; never worked from memory. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Established what actually happened, and from whom, asking the author where unclear. · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

- [ ] **Drafted and sent distinguished:** a drafted approach left the row at its first status; only the author sending it moved it on, dated to when the author sent it. · _opus_
- [ ] Every column filled; the status is one the tracker's `## Rules` list. · _sonnet_
- [ ] **Reply recorded exactly as it came, not upgraded;** its operative words quoted. · _opus_
- [ ] Anything received stored verbatim, attributed and dated, where the anatomy guide says. · _opus_
- [ ] A yes or an offer reported to the author at once. · _opus_
- [ ] **Every open row has a next action and a date,** following the anatomy guide's chase rule, or is closed. · _opus_
- [ ] Corrections appended under `## Corrections` with a date; no history overwritten. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Where the package's account of its readers disagrees with the tracker, the part flagged for `01-assemble-the-proposal`. · _opus_
- [ ] Handed back: one line per change, **plus everything falling due soon**. · _opus_

## Done When

- [ ] **The tracker reflects what actually happened, not what was drafted or hoped.** · _opus_
- [ ] No status is more favourable than the reply supports. · _opus_
- [ ] Every open row has a next action and a date; closed rows are closed. · _opus_
- [ ] The author knows what falls due next, and when. · _opus_
