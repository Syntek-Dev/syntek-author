## Purpose (one line)

Keep the tracker true: what happened, not what was drafted or hoped, with every open row's next
action dated.

## How to work here

**Follow `STEPS.md` in order and tick `CHECKLIST.md` as you go.** The model tags in the checklist
are authoritative: `opus` items are judgement; `sonnet` items belong to the mechanical tier.

- **Routing:** no skill; this is record-keeping, done by hand against the tracker's own rules.
  Guide: the package anatomy in `proposal/docs/reference/`.
- **Model:** the mechanical tier is fine for adding a row or a date; **Opus** for anything needing
  judgement: whether a reply is a yes, what the next action should be
  (`.claude/rules/syntek-author/05-model-allocation.md`).
- **Concrete steps:** read the tracker → establish what happened → update or create the row →
  record the reply as it came → keep anything received verbatim → report a yes or an offer at once
  → set the next action and date → append corrections → reconcile the package → hand back.
- **Definition of done:** the tracker reflects what happened; no status is more favourable than the
  reply supports; every open row has a next action and a date; nothing was overwritten.

## Guardrails

- **Log what happened, not what was drafted.** Only the author sending an approach moves its row
  on, dated to when the author sent it.
- **Record a reply exactly as it came, never upgraded.** 'Interested, will read in the spring' is
  not a yes; a request for a partial is not an offer.
- **Every open row has a next action and a date,** or it is closed. A row with neither is how
  someone who said 'ask me again in the spring' is forgotten.
- **Never overwrite history.** Corrections are appended and dated: who was asked what, when, and
  what was promised may matter later, to the author, to a publisher or to the reader.
- **Received words are kept verbatim,** attributed and dated, and never edited without the
  person's agreement.
- **The tracker outranks every other file.** If the package's account of its readers disagrees with
  the tracker, the package needs updating, not the tracker.

## Output & naming

- **Produces:** the updated tracker; anything received, stored where the anatomy guide says.
- **Does not produce:** emails (that is `02-approach-a-reader`), or any change to the pitch itself.
