## Execution Checklist

**Choosing**

- [ ] What this reader's yes unlocks stated. · _opus_
- [ ] **The specific reason it is this person named;** if none could be, the approach was stopped. · _opus_
- [ ] The reader's published requirements read and recorded in their row. · _opus_
- [ ] Anything offered exists and is promoted, or a realistic date is given, or the approach waits. · _opus_

**Writing**

- [ ] Started from the master and **tailored**; no untailored master. · _opus_
- [ ] Why them · the book, leading with the hook · the author's position in a clause where it matters · the ask with effort and date · an easy exit where the form allows · nothing else. · _opus_
- [ ] Within the length the anatomy sets, or the reader's own requirement. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Addressed to a named person, never a department where a name is available. · _opus_
- [ ] **Nothing invented:** no endorsement, interest, offer, deadline or unchecked comparable title. · _opus_
- [ ] `spelling` and `grammar` run as a supportive report; name, title and affiliation checked. · _opus_

**Handing over**

- [ ] Draft saved as `<reader-slug>.md` in the `drafts/` folder beside the tracker; nothing overwritten. · _sonnet_
- [ ] **Handed to the author. Nothing sent.** · _opus_
- [ ] Tracker updated through `03-update-the-tracker` only once the author confirmed it was sent. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] The approach would make its recipient feel personally asked, not mail-merged. · _opus_
- [ ] The ask is unambiguous and declining is easy. · _opus_
- [ ] Nothing was invented and nothing was sent. · _opus_
