# STEPS.md — approach a reader

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

The ordered procedure for drafting one approach to one reader. Short, and run often. Each step
names the skill and guide it uses. **Run in order** — the ordering is load-bearing — and tick
`CHECKLIST.md` as you go.

> Read the read-order files first (this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`), then
> `proposal/docs/reference/approaching-readers.md` and the package anatomy guide beside it. The
> `approach-a-reader` skill is this procedure in skill form.

## 1. Read the tracker

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** `proposal/docs/reference/approaching-readers.md`

The tracker's path is in `proposal/src/CONTEXT.md`. Read it **before anything else.** If this
person already has a row, you are revising or chasing: never draft a second first approach.
_Mechanical._

## 2. Confirm why this reader

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** `proposal/docs/reference/approaching-readers.md`

Whose yes unlocks which audience, and which objection does it answer? Then the specific reason it
is this person: their work, or the books on their list, that bear on this one. **If you cannot
name it, stop** and tell the author why. _Substantive._

## 3. Check what they ask for, and what exists to send

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** the package anatomy in `proposal/docs/reference/`

Read the reader's own published requirements, where they have them, and record them in their
tracker row. Check `manuscript/src/` and `proposal/src/sample/sample-index.md`: offer only what is
promoted, or give a realistic date, or wait. _Substantive._

## 4. Start from the master, then tailor

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** the package anatomy in `proposal/docs/reference/`

Begin from the master text the anatomy guide names, then tailor it to this person. **A master sent
untailored is the failure this procedure exists to prevent.** _Substantive._

## 5. Write the approach

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** `proposal/docs/reference/approaching-readers.md`

The moves: why them, specifically · the book briefly, leading with the hook · the author's
position, plainly, in a clause where it matters · the ask with its effort and date, or exactly what
their requirements ask for · an easy, sincere exit where the form allows · nothing else. Keep to the
length the anatomy sets. _Substantive._

## 6. Check for anything invented

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** `proposal/docs/reference/approaching-readers.md`

No claimed endorsement, no implied interest, no manufactured deadline, no sample that does not
exist, no comparable title unchecked. Each is checkable, and each ends a relationship.
_Substantive._

## 7. Proofread

> **Skill:** `spelling` · **Guide:** `proposal/docs/reference/approaching-readers.md`

Run `spelling` and `grammar` as a supportive report. Check the person's name, title and
affiliation against their own published page. This carries the author's name to someone they
respect. _Substantive._

## 8. Save the draft

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** `proposal/src/CONTEXT.md`

Save it as `<reader-slug>.md` in the `drafts/` folder beside the tracker. **Never overwrite an
existing draft** without confirming. _Mechanical._

## 9. Hand it to the author — do not send

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** `proposal/docs/reference/approaching-readers.md`

Report the draft's location, why this reader, the ask, what is offered and by when, and anything
needing the author's confirmation. **The author sends it.** _Substantive._

## 10. Log it once it has gone

> **Skill:** — · **Guide:** `proposal/workflows/03-update-the-tracker/STEPS.md`

When the author confirms it was sent, run `proposal/workflows/03-update-the-tracker/` with the date
the author sent it. Until then the row stays at its first status. _Mechanical._

## 11. When the reply does not come

> **Skill:** `approach-a-reader` · **Guide:** the package anatomy in `proposal/docs/reference/`

Follow the chase rule the anatomy guide sets, and no more: one chase, shorter than the original and
with an easier exit, where chasing is allowed at all; then the row is marked lapsed through
`03-update-the-tracker`. A second chase costs more goodwill than the yes is worth. _Substantive._
