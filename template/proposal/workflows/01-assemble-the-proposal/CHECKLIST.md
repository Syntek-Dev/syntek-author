# CHECKLIST.md — assemble the proposal

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** the package anatomy guide in `proposal/docs/reference/`,
> `proposal/docs/reference/comp-titles.md` and the `build` skill. Evidence gates are set by
> `standards/verification/verification.md`; this list never restates them.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Read the package anatomy guide and `comp-titles.md`. · _opus_
- [ ] **Open decisions named, and put to the author;** none made on the author's behalf. · _opus_
- [ ] Checked `manuscript/src/` and the briefs for which units have every section promoted. · _sonnet_

## Execution Checklist

**The pitch**

- [ ] Every part the anatomy lists located at its path in `proposal/src/CONTEXT.md`, with its state noted. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Pitch drafted with the author in the book's voice; it promises no more than the book delivers. · _opus_
- [ ] Comparable titles searched by shelf; **author, title, publisher, year and in-print status verified at the source**. · _opus_
- [ ] Every comparable title carries a one-line differentiator; strong ones named honestly. · _opus_
- [ ] The book's summary written for the reader who will receive it, not lifted from the briefs, and checked against the book as it stands. · _opus_
- [ ] Author section in true tenses; every claim checked or put to the author; **no employer, client or person named without the author's confirmation**. · _opus_

**Sources and sample**

- [ ] Every market, 'why now', platform or reach claim verified through `02-verify-a-claim`; anything `cannot-be-dated` or `unsupported` cut. · _opus_
- [ ] Sample recommended with reasons; the author chose, and the choice is recorded in `.claude/MEMORY.md` `## Decisions` (mapped in `00-project.md` `## Memory headings`). · _opus_
- [ ] One row in `sample-index.md` per chosen unit, and only for units whose sections are all promoted. · _sonnet_
- [ ] No manuscript prose copied into the package. · _sonnet_

**Proofing**

- [ ] `spelling` and `grammar` run as a supportive report; only accepted changes applied. · _opus_
- [ ] Each part built with its `SCOPE=`; the echoed file list checked. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Every proof read, not just built. · _opus_

## Done When

- [ ] **A reader could say yes on the strength of it, or would know exactly what is still coming.** · _opus_
- [ ] Nothing invented anywhere; every claim resolves to a checked source. · _opus_
- [ ] The open decisions sit with the author, clearly stated. · _opus_
- [ ] Handed back with proof paths and what is blocked. **Nothing sent.** · _opus_
