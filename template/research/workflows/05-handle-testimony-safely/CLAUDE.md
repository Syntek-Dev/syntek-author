## Purpose (one line)

Work with first-person testimony safely: in its owner's voice, with recorded consent, protecting
anyone named, and never towards print without the person's explicit decision.

## How to work here

**Follow `STEPS.md` in order and tick `CHECKLIST.md` as you go.** The model tags in the checklist
are authoritative: `opus` items are judgement; `sonnet` items belong to the mechanical tier.

- **Routing:** skill `sensitivity-pass` (a reviewer: it reports and never silently rewrites
  testimony); `spelling` and `grammar` for a supportive proofread of a settled note; guide
  `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`; standard
  `standards/risk/sensitive-content.md`.
- **Model:** **Opus** for everything here; nothing in this procedure is mechanical except a
  filename and a tick (`.claude/rules/syntek-author/05-model-allocation.md`).
- **Concrete steps:** read in → check readiness → open and preserve → shape in the owner's voice →
  name hard truths with care → protect the people named → record consent → run the sensitivity
  pass → plan the signpost → proofread supportively → hand on only what consent allows → close out.
- **Definition of done:** the note holds the voice faithfully; consent is explicit, specific, dated
  and recorded; the sensitivity pass is complete with blocking issues cleared or referred; people
  named are protected; any passage that could stir a reader is flagged for a signpost. It is never
  done if the person whose story it is has not chosen it.

## Guardrails

- **Consent is load-bearing.** Nothing is published verbatim, or paraphrased into print, without the
  person's explicit, considered decision. When in doubt, do not use it: ask rather than decide.
- **The voice is theirs.** Do not novelise, dramatise or ventriloquise; never smooth the words into
  generic prose. Clarify only with agreement.
- **Name hard truths with care.** The test is 'this names a hard truth with care', never 'this
  exposes'. No sensationalism, and no disclosure for its own sake: each must serve the reader.
- **Protect the people named.** Anonymise or generalise third parties by default; no child is ever
  identifiable; flag anything identifying or legally sensitive for the author's decision.
- **Hold the author's wellbeing in view.** Pace hard material gently; pause, flag and defer rather
  than push through.
- **Testimony is not a citable source.** No statistics and no claims about others are added to it.
- **Never overwrite an existing note or draft** without confirming with the author.

## Output & naming

- **Produces:** notes in `research/src/testimony/`, plainly named, each with its consent record.
- **Also writes:** the sensitivity report, returned to the author rather than filed.
- **Does not touch:** the manuscript itself. Drafting from consented material happens there,
  through the manuscript's own drafting procedure, only after this one is complete.
