# STEPS.md — handle testimony safely

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **Safeguarding-critical.** This procedure works with someone's own story. Consent is
> load-bearing; the author's wellbeing comes first; nothing reaches print without the explicit,
> considered decision of the person whose story it is.

The ordered procedure for working with first-person testimony. Each step names the skill and guide
it uses. **Run in order** — the ordering is load-bearing — and tick `CHECKLIST.md` as you go.

> Read `standards/risk/sensitive-content.md` first, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and
> `CLAUDE.md`, then `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`. The `sensitivity-pass` skill is
> the review this procedure turns on.

## 1. Read in before you open anything

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`

Read the standard, `.claude/MEMORY.md` (Sensitivities, mapped in `00-project.md`
`## Memory headings`), the guide, and `research/src/testimony/CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. Do not
open a note until this is done. _Substantive._

## 2. Check the author's readiness

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`

Confirm the author is willing to work on this material now. This is never done alone: the author's
own support stays in the picture, and the work never pretends to be it. If there is any doubt,
pause and say so. _Substantive._

## 3. Open the note, and preserve the original

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `research/src/testimony/CONTEXT.md`

Locate the note in `research/src/testimony/`, or agree with the author where a new one will sit and
what it is called. Read it through once, whole, before marking anything. Never overwrite it: any
change is a dated addition, or a working copy saved beside it, plainly named. _Substantive._

## 4. Shape it in the owner's voice

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`

Capture, order and gently clarify the words, with the author, never over them. Do not rewrite,
sharpen, novelise or 'improve' them into publishable prose; clarify only with agreement.
_Substantive._

## 5. Name hard truths with care

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `standards/risk/sensitive-content.md`

Apply the test: 'this names a hard truth with care', never 'this exposes'. No sensationalism, and
no disclosure for its own sake: every one must earn its place by serving the reader. _Substantive._

## 6. Protect the people named

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`

Anonymise or generalise third parties by default, remembering that a person can be identified by
role, place or timing. No child is ever identifiable. Flag anything identifying or legally
sensitive for the author's decision rather than deciding it. _Substantive._

## 7. Record consent

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `research/src/testimony/CONTEXT.md`

Ask, and record the answer in the note's frontmatter: `consent`, `consent_date` and `hard_lines`,
in the person's own terms. For an entrusted account, the teller's consent is recorded, and the
teller appears only under a pseudonym the author chooses. Never infer consent from silence or from
an earlier yes. _Substantive._

## 8. Run the sensitivity pass

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `standards/risk/sensitive-content.md`

Run `sensitivity-pass` over any passage that consent allows to move towards a chapter. It reports;
it **never rewrites testimony**. Resolve its blocking issues with the author, or refer them to the
author. _Substantive._

## 9. Plan the signpost

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `standards/risk/sensitive-content.md`

Where a passage could stir a reader, or the author, flag it so the chapter ends near a route to
support in the form the standard sets. Never name a source of help from memory. _Substantive._

## 10. Proofread supportively, if asked

> **Skill:** `spelling` · **Guide:** `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`

For a settled note the author has agreed, run `spelling` and `grammar` as a report, not a rewrite:
say what and where, offer the correction, and change nothing in the voice. _Substantive._

## 11. Hand on only what consent allows

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`

Only when the author says so, and only within the recorded consent, name the passages that may
shape a chapter and add the chapter slugs to `serves`. Drafting happens in the manuscript, through
its own drafting procedure; nothing is drafted here. _Substantive._

## 12. Close out

> **Skill:** `sensitivity-pass` · **Guide:** `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`

Tick `CHECKLIST.md`, then report to the author: what was recorded, what consent allows, what the
sensitivity pass found, and what waits on their decision. Nothing is called done that the author
has not chosen. _Substantive._
