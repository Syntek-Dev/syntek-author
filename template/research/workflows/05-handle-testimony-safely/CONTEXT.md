# CONTEXT.md — research/workflows/05-handle-testimony-safely/

> **Safeguarding-critical.** Read `standards/risk/sensitive-content.md` before opening anything in
> `research/src/testimony/`. Consent is load-bearing; the author's wellbeing comes first.

The safeguarding procedure for `research/src/testimony/`: first-person material, whether the
author's own story or an account entrusted to the author. It exists so that this material is
handled the same careful way every time: with the author, never over them; protecting anyone named;
holding the author's wellbeing in view; and letting nothing move towards the manuscript without the
explicit, considered decision of the person whose story it is.

## When to use this

Whenever testimony is touched or moved, including when you:

- capture, order or gently clarify a new or existing note with the author;
- record what the person whose story it is will and will not make public, and where a hard line
  sits;
- prepare a testimony-derived passage to shape a chapter, which needs recorded consent and a
  completed `sensitivity-pass` first;
- give a settled note a supportive proofread.

Reach for a **different** procedure for evidence, statistics or third-party sources
(`research/workflows/01-ingest-a-source/`, `research/workflows/02-verify-a-claim/`). Testimony is
not evidence, and evidence procedures never open this folder.

## What it produces, and where

- **A note** in `research/src/testimony/`, in the voice of the person whose story it is, with its
  consent record in the frontmatter.
- **A sensitivity report** from `sensitivity-pass`, with blocking issues resolved or referred to the
  author.
- **A hand-on** to the manuscript of only what consent allows, flagged for a signpost where it
  could stir a reader.

## What it must never do

Move anything towards print by inference, default or momentum. Consent is explicit, specific and
recorded, or it is absent. A procedure that is 'nearly done' when the author has not chosen is not
done at all.
