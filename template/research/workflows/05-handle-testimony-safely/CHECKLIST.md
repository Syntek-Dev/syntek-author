# CHECKLIST.md — handle testimony safely

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** `standards/risk/sensitive-content.md`, `research/docs/reference/handling-testimony.md`
> and the `sensitivity-pass` skill. Gates are set by `standards/verification/verification.md`;
> this list never restates them.
>
> **Safeguarding-critical.** Consent is load-bearing; the author's wellbeing comes first; nothing
> reaches print without the explicit, considered decision of the person whose story it is.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] **Read `standards/risk/sensitive-content.md` in full for this task.** · _opus_
- [ ] Read `.claude/MEMORY.md` (Sensitivities), the guide, and `research/src/testimony/CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _opus_
- [ ] Author confirmed willing to work on this material now; their own support in the picture. · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

**Opening**

- [ ] Note located, or its home and name agreed with the author. · _opus_
- [ ] Note read through once, whole, before anything was marked. · _opus_
- [ ] Original preserved: no existing note overwritten; any working copy saved beside it, plainly named. · _sonnet_

**Shaping**

- [ ] Words captured, ordered and gently clarified, never rewritten, novelised or smoothed. · _opus_
- [ ] Clarifications made only with agreement; the voice preserved. · _opus_
- [ ] Hard truths named with care; no sensationalism, no disclosure for its own sake. · _opus_
- [ ] Third parties anonymised or generalised unless explicitly cleared; no child identifiable. · _opus_
- [ ] Anything identifying or legally sensitive flagged for the author's decision. · _opus_

**Consent and review**

- [ ] **Consent recorded in the frontmatter, explicit and dated, in the person's own terms**, with any hard lines. · _opus_
- [ ] For an entrusted account: the teller's consent recorded, and the teller named only by pseudonym. · _opus_
- [ ] `sensitivity-pass` run; it reported and did not rewrite testimony. · _opus_
- [ ] Blocking issues resolved with the author or referred to the author. · _opus_
- [ ] Any passage that could stir a reader flagged for a signpost; no source of help named from memory. · _opus_
- [ ] Any proofread was a supportive report from `spelling` and `grammar`, with no change to the voice. · _opus_
- [ ] Nothing added to the note as evidence: no statistics, no claims about others. · _opus_

## Done When

- [ ] **Nothing moved towards a chapter beyond what the recorded consent allows, and only on the author's word.** · _opus_
- [ ] The note holds its owner's voice faithfully. · _opus_
- [ ] The people named are protected. · _opus_
- [ ] The author's wellbeing was held in view throughout; nothing was pushed through. · _opus_
- [ ] The author has the report: what was recorded, what consent allows, what waits on them. · _opus_
