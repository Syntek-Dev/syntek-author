The register of every passage the novel reproduces from another work (epigraphs, quoted lines, song lyrics, poems, a text a character reads aloud) and the state of its clearance.
Treat anything from a work still in copyright as needing permission until the publisher says otherwise.

## How to add a row

- One row per quoted passage, added when the passage enters a draft, not at the end.
- **Status:** `to check` · `not needed` · `to request` · `requested` · `granted` · `refused` · `cut`.
- `not needed` carries its basis in Notes (for example, the work is out of copyright, with the date that was checked) and is confirmed by the publisher before it is relied on.
- Never record a permission as granted without the grant in hand; file it with the date it came.
- Dates are DD/MM/YYYY.
- Corrections are appended below the register with a date; history is never overwritten.
