# CHECKLIST.md — create a business document

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** `library/docs/reference/business-standards.md` and the `business-documents` skill. Gates
> are those of `standards/verification/verification.md`, named by the transition they guard; this
> list never restates them.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] `business-documents` loaded; `library/docs/reference/business-standards.md` read. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Confirmed with the author: the document's type, its reader, and what it must achieve. · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

**Before planning**

- [ ] Register, briefs and the client's folder searched; a circulated document of the same type opened as a new version instead. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Earlier documents to the same client read. · _opus_
- [ ] Client folder found, or created with its pair and `## Facts` from the public register and the author, each fact dated and sourced. · _opus_
- [ ] Place and name set to the standard's pattern; nothing at that path overwritten. · _sonnet_

**Planning**

- [ ] Brief planned through `planning/workflows/01-plan-a-unit/`, its sections covering every required part for the type. · _opus_
- [ ] **Scope, price and dates settled with the author, not supplied by the AI.** · _opus_
- [ ] V1 (idea → outlined) dated before any drafting. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Starting point chosen: a template adapted through `library/workflows/02-adapt-a-draft/`, or the house skeleton. · _sonnet_

**Writing and review**

- [ ] Each section run through `library/workflows/01-draft-a-section/` (or `library/workflows/03-improve-your-draft/`) and promoted through `library/workflows/04-promote-a-section/` on the author's word. · _opus_
- [ ] `library/workflows/05-review-a-document/` run in full, with the standard's required parts checked at structure review. · _opus_
- [ ] Disclaimer present where the class carries one; investment as line items; scope with In and Out. · _opus_

**Hand-back**

- [ ] Issue PDF beside the `.tex`; document listed in its client folder's `CONTEXT.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Handed back: path, register ID, status, anything outstanding, any step waived and why. · _opus_

## Done When

- [ ] **The document is `final` on the author's word, at its named path, with every part its type requires.** · _opus_
- [ ] Every section came through the loop and has its ledger entry. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The register row exists and the issue PDF sits beside the `.tex`. · _sonnet_
