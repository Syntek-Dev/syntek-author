# CHECKLIST.md — improve a section the author wrote

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** `library/docs/reference/drafting-with-ai.md` and the `improve-section` skill. Gates are
> those of `standards/verification/verification.md`, named by the transition they guard; this list
> never restates them.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Section and strength confirmed with the author: `light`, `edit` or `rework`. · _opus_
- [ ] The draft is on record as a file at `author-draft` with a ledger entry; nothing overwritten. · _sonnet_

## Execution Checklist

**Reading**

- [ ] Brief line, voice notes (with `## Learned`), terminology, samples and neighbouring sections read. · _opus_
- [ ] Earlier ledger decisions read; no rejected change proposed again. · _opus_

**Proposing**

- [ ] Structural pass done at `edit` or `rework` only; every point and obligation kept. · _opus_
- [ ] Register checked against the document's kind and the business's person. · _opus_
- [ ] Each proposal numbered, with the original line, the proposed line and one line of reason. · _opus_
- [ ] **No proposal touches a figure, date, price, scope boundary, defined term or commitment; each such concern raised as a question.** · _opus_
- [ ] Proofreading report: what and where, the correction offered, recurring items grouped. · _opus_

**Deciding and recording**

- [ ] The author's decision received on every proposal and correction. · _opus_
- [ ] Exactly the accepted changes applied, nothing else; one sentence per line kept. · _sonnet_
- [ ] One ledger row per proposal, accepted or rejected, with the author's note. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Status set to `improved` (if anything was accepted); `last_updated` set; brief mirrored. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Handed back: counts accepted and rejected, open questions, flags left, next move. · _opus_

## Done When

- [ ] **The draft carries exactly the changes the author accepted, and no others.** · _opus_
- [ ] No commitment, figure or date has changed through this procedure. · _opus_
- [ ] Every proposal and its outcome is in the ledger. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The draft stays in its drafts folder; promotion waits for the author's word. · _opus_
