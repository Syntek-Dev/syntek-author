# CHECKLIST.md — write an email

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>

> **See** `library/docs/reference/email-standards.md`, its sub-document and the `email-documents`
> skill. Gates are those of `standards/verification/verification.md`, named by the transition they
> guard; this list never restates them.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] `email-documents` loaded, then the skill of the governing family. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Confirmed with the author: the correspondent, the matter, the one ask. · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

**Before writing**

- [ ] Family leaf decided by the engagement that owns the matter; the call noted if not obvious. · _opus_
- [ ] Correspondent's facts and recorded preferences read; archived threads on the matter read. · _opus_
- [ ] **Every other unsent email to the same correspondent read together;** contradictions listed for the author. · _opus_
- [ ] Folder found, or created with its pair on the author's confirmation. · _sonnet_
- [ ] One-section brief planned; V1 (idea → outlined) dated. · _opus_

**Writing**

- [ ] Body drafted through `library/workflows/01-draft-a-section/` (or improved through `library/workflows/03-improve-your-draft/`). · _opus_
- [ ] Leads with the decision or the ask; nothing cut that was an ask, a caveat, a figure or a commitment. · _opus_
- [ ] A follow-up recites no undertaking, counts no time and denies no chasing. · _opus_
- [ ] Subject settled (50 characters or fewer, 60 at most); file named from it. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Fixed parts written: frontmatter, title, metadata, internal note, rule, subject, marker pair. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Body promoted on the author's word through `library/workflows/04-promote-a-section/`. · _sonnet_

**Review and hand-back**

- [ ] `library/workflows/05-review-a-document/` run; every commitment traced to its instrument. · _opus_
- [ ] Email checklist in `EMAIL-ANATOMY-AND-NAMING.md` worked through; no em dashes; no unflagged placeholder. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Listed in its folder's `CONTEXT.md` and registered; any paste copy regenerated. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Handed back: path, subject, flags, contradictions; `**Status:**` left at Draft. · _opus_

## Done When

- [ ] **The email waits at Draft, filed by correspondent and family, consistent with every other unsent email to them.** · _opus_
- [ ] It carries no commitment the instruments do not, and no clause number. · _opus_
- [ ] It is listed and registered. · _sonnet_
