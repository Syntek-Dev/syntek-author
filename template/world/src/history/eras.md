The eras of the world, oldest first: the spine every event file in `world/src/history/` hangs on.
The story's own days live in `planning/src/timeline.md`; this file holds the long past that shaped the peoples, cultures and languages the story meets.

## Writing rules

1. **One row per era, oldest first.**
   An era is a stretch of time with one defining condition (a homeland held, an empire standing, a language still undivided).
2. **`Span` uses the world's own reckoning**, stated once under Reckoning.
   'About' is allowed, and relative spans ('ten generations') are allowed when nothing fixes an absolute one.
3. **`Peoples` lists the slugs of people files** present in the era, in `world/src/peoples/`.
4. **`Language stage` names the speech each people used in the era** (an undivided parent language early on, its daughters after a split), or a dash when nobody has decided.
5. **`Events` lists the slugs of the event files** that fall within the era, in order.
6. **One sentence per cell.**
