## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The people's job in the story agreed with the author, including whether the story casts them as hostile. · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

**Before inventing anything**

- [ ] Read the register, the other peoples, the cultures, the eras and events, the places and `planning/src/continuity.md`. · _opus_
- [ ] Name chosen by the author, or a working name agreed and flagged. · _opus_

**The file**

- [ ] Checked `world/src/peoples/<slug>.md` does not exist; if it did, stopped and asked. · _sonnet_
- [ ] File created from the skeleton, one sentence per line, `first_appears` empty. · _sonnet_
- [ ] **Body and speech settled and written, 'human, no constraint' where nothing differs; never left blank.** · _opus_
- [ ] Lifespan, generations, numbers and spread settled, with what the lifespan means for change. · _opus_
- [ ] Homelands as place slugs, oldest first; every movement named by event slug or listed for charting. · _opus_
- [ ] Relations to each depicted people settled; relations that changed either side listed for charting. · _opus_
- [ ] Every fact chosen by the author and recorded as it resolved; undecided points flagged. · _opus_

**Depiction**

- [ ] Borrowings from real peoples recorded and checked against `standards/risk/FICTION.md`. · _opus_
- [ ] A people cast as hostile checked for any recognisable real group; alternatives offered; concerns reported. · _opus_
- [ ] The outcome written in the note under the frontmatter. · _sonnet_

**Recording**

- [ ] The name registered with kind `people`, or left flagged while it is a working name. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] **A culture or a language for this people could be built without guessing at their bodies, lifespan or past.** · _opus_
- [ ] The depiction note is filled. · _opus_
- [ ] Handed back: file, name, depiction outcome, events to chart, cultures to describe, contradictions, open flags. · _opus_
