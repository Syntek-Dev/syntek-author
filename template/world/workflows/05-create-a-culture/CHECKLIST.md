# CHECKLIST.md — create a culture

**Last Updated**: <%DATE%> **Version**: 0.1.0 **Maintained By**: <%AUTHOR_NAME%>
**Language**: British English (en_GB)

> **See** `world/docs/reference/cultures.md`, `world/docs/reference/naming.md`,
> `standards/risk/FICTION.md` and the `create-name` skill. Gates cite
> `standards/verification/verification.md` by number; this list never restates them.

## Pre-Conditions

- [ ] Read `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/MEMORY.md`, then this folder's `CONTEXT.md` and `CLAUDE.md`. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The culture's job in the story agreed with the author. · _opus_

## Execution Checklist

**Before inventing anything**

- [ ] Read the register, the people's file, the existing cultures, the history, the people's places and characters, and `planning/src/continuity.md`; a missing people file offered first. · _opus_
- [ ] Where the people speak a constructed language, its grammar and phonology read. · _opus_

**The culture**

- [ ] **Land and livelihood settled before any value or custom.** · _opus_
- [ ] Materials and tools recorded, including what they would write on and with. · _opus_
- [ ] Values, taboos and beliefs each tied to what the land demanded; deep-vocabulary domains and sacred words named. · _opus_
- [ ] Power, kinship, forms of address and customs recorded, each custom with its reason. · _opus_
- [ ] Naming customs written and tested on five sample names from `create-name`; customs revised if the samples did not cohere. · _opus_
- [ ] At least one internal division recorded, and relations with each depicted neighbour. · _opus_
- [ ] Borrowings from real peoples recorded and checked against `standards/risk/FICTION.md`; concerns reported. · _opus_
- [ ] Every value, custom and name chosen by the author; undecided points flagged. · _opus_

**Writing it down**

- [ ] Checked `world/src/cultures/<slug>.md` does not exist; if it did, stopped and asked. · _sonnet_
- [ ] Culture file written from the skeleton, one sentence per line, `people` filled, `first_appears` empty. · _sonnet_
- [ ] The culture's own name registered (kind `culture`) only where it differs from its people's; the people's name never registered twice; only the sample names the book will use added. · _sonnet_

## Done When

- [ ] **A character from this people could be written without inventing a custom.** · _opus_
- [ ] The depiction check is recorded in the file. · _opus_
- [ ] Handed back: file, names, depiction outcome, contradicting characters or places, open flags. · _opus_
